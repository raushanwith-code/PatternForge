// why-patterns.md

Grinding LeetCode randomly doesn't work.

There are 3,000+ problems on LeetCode. Nobody has time to solve them all — and they don't need to. Almost every problem is a variation of one of ~25 recurring patterns. Learn the pattern, and you can recognize it in an interview even when the problem is dressed up differently.

◆

Pattern-first, not problem-first

Every problem is tagged to the technique it teaches — two pointers, sliding window, backtracking — so you build transferable intuition, not memorized answers.

◆

Curated, not exhaustive

Up to 15 hand-picked problems per pattern — enough to internalize the shape without burning weeks on redundant repeats of the same idea.

◆

Progress that persists

Sign in once and every solved problem is saved to your account — pick up your prep exactly where you left off, on any device.

◆

Direct to the source

Every problem links straight to the real LeetCode page — no paywalled clones, no rewritten prompts, the actual problem you'll see in an interview.
